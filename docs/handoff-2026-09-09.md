Preserve existing behavior that closes trays and toasts on navigation.

`activity_art.rs`. Kodi's existing pixel cache has no TTL/config invalidation;
review that while implementing the shared behavior.

## IR remains unresolved

The driver completes DMA at the expected rate, but the LG B4 did not respond to
single Volume Up frames, repeated full frames, or full frames followed by NEC
ditto bursts. The repeat-spacing defect is fixed (`70f4924`); that alone did not
resolve reception. No further blind transmissions are warranted.

The actual original ROM/kernel/HAL audit found no omitted GPIO, regulator or
LED-enable sequence. The stock kernel disables the generic IR-learning driver;
a HAL callback does not prove an onboard receiver exists. Optical emission and
signal quality still need a known-sensitive IR receiver/camera or scope. See
[IR status](ir.md) and [stock ROM audit](ha100-stock-ir-audit.md).

The installed diagnostic kernel is `3.18.79-couch-normal-gb3c10e0eb779`.
Kernel builds must run on Ollie. Preserve recovery and identity backups.
