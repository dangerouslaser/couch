Three directions for your LG TV. Sources, picture and sound within reach.
Artwork and a timeline when the linked player can supply them.

A · Cinema / recommended

Full-bleed artwork flows into a quiet playback deck. Input, picture and sound stay one tap away. With TV-only information, it becomes an atmospheric source screen without inventing a movie title.

B · Source deck

The current source has the stage. A horizontal input rail makes switching devices immediate; a compact media card keeps the film visible. Best if switching between Mac, CoreELEC and apps is common.

C · Dashboard

A denser tool surface: current media, favorite apps and a settings grid. Most useful when picture and sound adjustments matter more than cinematic presentation.

Interactive design studies, 480 × 800. All interactions are simulated; no device commands are sent.
Movie metadata/art are illustrative linked-Kodi data, not data returned by your TV. Picture presets are unverified on this pairing.
