A little cinema.
In your hand.

Three full-screen directions for Kodi. Artwork sets the mood.
Playback stays within reach.

MY PICK

A · Cinema

The backdrop and clearlogo make the activity feel like its own place. Keep the controls grounded on a dark gradient, with chapters and audio in a temporary sheet.

TRY IT

More than a picture.

Play/pause, scrub, open chapters, change audio or captions, and try TV controls. Arrow keys move focus; Enter selects; Escape goes back; Space toggles playback. Exit returns to a simulated room without stopping the film.

480 × 800

Built around the remote.

Every canvas matches the panel. No room-list header or persistent bottom navigation. Artwork is static; this is a controller, not a video stream. In the product, volume keys follow the activity’s configured audio device.

Interactive design prototype · Simulated playback, chapter names and streams · No connection to your devices.
