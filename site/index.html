LESS FUSS. MORE LIVING.

A remote.
On your terms.

01 / FEEL

Buttons where they belong.

Navigate, adjust the volume, and control playback by feel. The touchscreen makes room for your devices, scenes, and richer media views.

02 / CONTROL

Your room, brought together.

Organize devices into rooms. Bring lights, players, TVs, and receivers together, with a browser-based configuration UI to make it yours.

03 / BUILD

Small device. Full ownership.

Rust integrations, a Slint interface, and a custom Linux kernel. Built for this hardware, with source you can explore and help improve.

A WORK IN PROGRESS

Pull up a seat.

Couch is running on development hardware and evolving quickly. The installer is experimental; a ready-to-flash public release isn’t available yet. Follow along, explore the code, or help shape what comes next.
