INTEGRATIONS

Supported integrations

Couch brings media players, lights, TVs and receivers together. Add connections in the web UI, put devices into rooms, and choose which device each activity button controls.

Support is under active development. The list below describes what is implemented today and calls out features that still need hardware validation.

Kodi

Media & activities

Navigate Kodi, control playback, and see library artwork, progress, chapters, audio and subtitles on the remote.

Using Kodi →

Philips Hue

Lights, rooms & scenes

Control lights and grouped rooms, adjust brightness, and recall scenes from your Hue bridge. Multiple bridges can be configured.

Lights & scenes →

Home Assistant

Lights

Add lights from your Home Assistant server to Couch rooms, then toggle and dim supported entities. Each server has its own connection.

LG webOS

TV controls & apps

Use physical navigation, volume, channel and color buttons. Choose TV inputs and apps from the screen; available settings depend on your television.

Denon AVR

Receiver controls

Adjust receiver volume, mute, power and inputs. Activity mappings can send audio controls to the receiver while navigation goes to another device.

Android / Google TV

Pairing, controls & app shortcuts

Pair using the TV’s on-screen code, then navigate and control playback. Add favorite app launch links as shortcuts. Artwork and a playback timeline are not available yet.

Apple TV

Experimental

Pair with an on-screen PIN for navigation, playback controls and an installed-app list. Real-device validation is still pending; now-playing artwork and a timeline are not included.

Built-in infrared

Transmission not yet working

Per-device infrared configuration is in the codebase. Sending commands from the remote’s built-in blaster still needs hardware bring-up.

Use integrations together

A room can combine devices from different integrations. Activities can route navigation to a media player, volume to a receiver, and lighting commands to a bridge.
